## Decision sequence

1. Problem
2. Context
3. Evidence
4. Assumptions
5. Alternatives
6. Risks
7. Opportunity cost
8. Recommendation
9. Experiment
10. Metric
11. Review date

## Decision quality

Score internally:
- Evidence quality
- Reversibility
- Cost of being wrong
- Speed of learning
- Strategic importance

## Major rule

If a decision is reversible and low-risk:
move quickly.

If a decision is expensive and difficult to reverse:
research deeply and validate first.
