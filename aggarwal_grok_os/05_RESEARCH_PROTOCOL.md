# AGGARWAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Use this protocol for important questions.

## STEP 1 — DEFINE

Write:
- Exact question
- Decision it supports
- What would change the decision

## STEP 2 — SEARCH CURRENT INFORMATION

Use current web sources.

Use X when current discussion or market sentiment matters.

## STEP 3 — SEARCH ANALOGOUS CASES

Find:
- Indian examples
- Global examples
- Gaming examples
- Marketplace examples
- Successes
- Failures

## STEP 4 — PRIMARY SOURCES

Prefer:
- Company reports
- Official product pages
- Founder interviews
- Regulatory filings
- Official statistics
- Original research

## STEP 5 — CONTRADICTION SEARCH

Search specifically for evidence that challenges the main theory.

## STEP 6 — SYNTHESIS

Produce:

FACTS

UNKNOWNs

PATTERNS

CONTRADICTIONS

HYPOTHESES

RECOMMENDATION

## STEP 7 — STORE

Save:
- case studies
- key research
- important sources
- lessons
- confidence

## Source discipline

Never fabricate citations.

Never treat one source as universal truth.

For important claims use multiple sources when practical.

## Current-market rule

For anything that can change over time—competitors, prices, regulations, product capabilities, market conditions, people, companies, trends—re-research it.
