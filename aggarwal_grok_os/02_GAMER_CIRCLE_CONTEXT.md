# GAMER CIRCLE — COMPANY SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Company

Gamer Circle

## Initial market

India

## Initial city/region

Delhi NCR

## Vision

Build a trusted gaming ecosystem where gamers can discover gaming venues, understand what is available right now, visit/book easily, and eventually participate in a broader gaming community.

## Primary customer groups

### Gamers

Needs may include:

- Nearby gaming venues
- Gaming PC availability
- Console availability
- Prices
- Photos
- Opening hours
- Facilities
- Reviews
- Directions
- Offers
- Booking
- Community

### Gaming venue owners

Needs may include:

- New customer acquisition
- Digital presence
- Accurate venue listing
- Availability management
- Promotions
- Booking management
- Customer management
- Analytics
- Repeat customers

## Potential core loop

Gamer:
Discover → Evaluate → Availability → Visit/Book → Good experience → Return

Venue:
List → Receive discovery → Receive customer → Measure value → Improve listing → Pay → Repeat

## Product direction

Potential progression:

1. Venue discovery
2. Trusted venue information
3. Nearby search
4. Availability
5. Booking
6. Payment
7. Community
8. Content
9. Venue SaaS
10. Ecosystem

Do not assume all layers must be built.

## Strategic risk

Avoid building too many features before proving:

- Gamer demand
- Venue supply
- Retention
- Marketplace liquidity
- Willingness to pay

## Current strategic question

What is the smallest product that proves a strong recurring need for gamers and measurable value for gaming venues in Delhi NCR?

## Founder working principle

Build evidence before complexity.
