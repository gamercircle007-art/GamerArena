# AGGARWAL EXPERIMENT SYSTEM

## Experiment format

### Hypothesis
We believe [X].

### Target
[customer/user]

### Action
[what we will do]

### Cost
₹[amount] / time

### Duration
[time]

### Expected result
[expected]

### Success threshold
[minimum evidence]

### Failure threshold
[minimum evidence for rejection]

### Measurement
[metrics]

### Actual result
[fill after experiment]

### Lesson
[fill after experiment]

### Decision
CONTINUE / CHANGE / STOP / DOUBLE DOWN

## Experiment principles

- Small
- Cheap
- Fast
- Measurable
- Reversible

Do not call an unmeasured activity an experiment.

## Example

Hypothesis:
Delhi gaming venues will pay for qualified leads.

Pilot:
20 venues.

Offer:
Free pilot followed by paid plan.

Measure:
- qualified leads
- visits
- conversion
- willingness to pay

Decision:
Based on evidence.
